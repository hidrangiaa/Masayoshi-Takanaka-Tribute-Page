Masayoshi in Summary:

Masayoshi is a Japanese guitarist/songwriter who began his career in bands, but found most of his fame through becoming a soloist. With his unique jazz-fusion, samba, and city pop music, Masayoshi was popular in Japan long before the western world really even know of his existence. Masayoshi became the foundation for the revival of city pop with his first album, "Seychelles" and remains popular in Japan, though he's begun to gain traction throughout the west.

Masayoshi's Life

  • Masayoshi was born Tokyo on March 27th, 1953.
  • By the time Takanaka was in middle school, he was picking up the guitar. He found inspiration in several western bands, like The Beatles.
  • In 1971, Takanaka began his career by playing guitar and bass with "Flied Egg", a prog rock band.
  • Just a year later, he joined "The Sadistic Mika Band" as a guitarist, and remained an active member of "The Sadistics" even after the original band fragmented.
  • With the Sadistic Mika Band, Masayoshi went on tour in the UK, making him apart of the first Japanese rock band to tour the UK.
  • He started his solo career with his album "Seychelles" in 1976.
  • 1979 Blue Lagoon becomes a hit single in Japan.
  • August 2,1981 Masayoshi performs live with Santana at the Yokohama baseball stadium.
  • In the 2000's, Masayoshi created his own personal record label called Lagoon Records.
  • November 2025, Masayoshi announced his first ever UK solo gig, being the first time in over 50 years he'd be in the UK.
